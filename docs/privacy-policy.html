Nihongo Path - Privacy Policy
Last updated: 16 November 2026

Nihongo Path helps you learn Japanese. We built it so that your data stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking.

1. Who is responsible

Artin Studios is the data fiduciary (India, DPDP Act 2023) and controller (EU GDPR) for how the app handles personal data. Grievance officer: Grievance Officer, Artin Studios - artinstudios.official@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores - only on your phone

  • Profile you enter: name, optional age range, reasons for learning, daily goal, pledge, personal goal.
  • Learning progress: XP, streaks, words known, quiz answers, study time per section.
  • Voice recordings you make (sound + pronunciation analysis) and handwriting you draw (strokes + scores).
  • Settings and backup preferences.

The developer never receives this data. It is kept in the app’s private storage until you delete it or uninstall the app.

3. Why

Only to run the app for you: show your progress, schedule reviews, give pronunciation and handwriting feedback, and back up your data when you ask. Your voice is used for feedback only - never to identify you.

4. What leaves your phone

  • Speech recognition (Speak aloud, word check) uses your phone’s speech service (usually Google), which may process audio on Google’s servers under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Links to YouTube and websites open in your browser or the YouTube app.
  • Backups you save go to your phone’s Downloads folder; you choose whether to copy them elsewhere. Android cloud backup may include progress and settings, never recordings or drawings.
  • Cloud backup (optional): if you save to Google Drive, OneDrive or another cloud app, that service stores the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Feedback and problem reports (optional) are sent only when you press send, through the app you choose (WhatsApp, email…). If you leave “Include device & app details” on, the message also contains your device model, Android and app version, screen size, language, install and join dates, and learning stats (level, XP, words, streak, study time) - you can see the full list before sending. Your IP address and location are never collected.
  • Share images (your level card, the invite card) are created on your phone and shared only where you choose.
  • Support payments (optional) are handled entirely by the payment app you choose (UPI, Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi); the app does not see payment details.

5. Your consent and your rights

You agree to this policy when you set up the app, and you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data. You can see your data in the app, export it (Settings → Backup), correct it (Settings), and erase it (Settings → Privacy → Delete). For any request or complaint, contact the grievance officer above; in India you may also approach the Data Protection Board of India, and in the EU your data protection authority.

6. Children

Learners under 18 need a parent or guardian’s consent to use voice recording. Without it the app runs in child mode: pronunciation analysis works but recordings and drawings are not saved, and support/payment links are hidden. The app never shows ads or tracks behaviour.

7. Security

Data stays in private app storage; the app uses no server and blocks unencrypted network traffic. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone may see your progress - use your phone’s screen lock.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the app shows the new version after an update.
