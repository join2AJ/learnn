# Nihongo Path - Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

Nihongo Path helps you learn Japanese. It is built so that **your data stays on your phone**: no account, no server, no advertising, no tracking.

## 1. Who is responsible
The publisher of Nihongo Path is the *data fiduciary* (India, Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023) and *controller* (EU GDPR) for how the app handles personal data.
**Developer:** Artin Studios - **Grievance officer:** Grievance Officer, Artin Studios - **Email:** artinstudios.official@gmail.com

## 2. What the app stores - only on your phone
- Profile you enter: name, optional age range, reasons for learning, daily goal, pledge, personal goal.
- Learning progress: XP, streaks, words known, quiz answers, study time per section.
- Voice recordings you make (sound + pronunciation analysis) and handwriting you draw (strokes + scores).
- Settings and backup preferences.

The developer never receives this data. It stays in the app's private storage until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## 3. Why
Only to run the app for you: show progress, schedule reviews, give pronunciation and handwriting feedback, and back up your data when you ask. Your voice is used **for feedback only - never to identify you**.

## 4. What leaves your phone
- **Speech recognition** (Speak aloud, word check) uses your phone's speech service (usually Google), which may process audio on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy.
- **Links** to YouTube and websites open in your browser or the YouTube app.
- **Backups** you save go to your phone's Downloads folder; you decide whether to copy them elsewhere. Android cloud backup may include progress and settings, never recordings or drawings.
- **Optional support payments** are handled entirely by the payment app you choose (UPI, Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi). The app never sees payment details.

## 5. Your consent and your rights
You agree to this policy when you set up the app and can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data. You can **access** your data in the app, **export** it (Settings → Backup), **correct** it (Settings) and **erase** it (Settings → Privacy → Delete). For any request or complaint, contact the grievance officer. In India you may also approach the Data Protection Board of India; in the EU, your data protection authority.

## 6. Children
Learners under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent to save voice recordings. Without it the app runs in **child mode**: pronunciation analysis still works, but recordings and drawings are not saved and support/payment links are hidden. The app never shows ads or tracks behaviour.

## 7. Security
Data stays in private app storage; the app has no server and blocks unencrypted network traffic. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone may see your progress, so please use a screen lock.

## 8. Changes
If this policy changes, the app shows the updated version after an update.
