Did you know?
Japanese grammar is closer to Hindi than to English
Same word order: subject → object → verb
EnglishI eat rice
हिन्दीमैं चावल खाता हूँ
日本語私は ご飯を 食べます
watashi wa gohan o tabemasu
Japanese even has “postpositions” like Hindi: は ≈ “ने/तो”, を ≈ “को”, で ≈ “में/से”.
